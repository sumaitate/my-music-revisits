## Interpretation and limits

At held-out k=25, production-score NDCG is 0.557, tuned-score NDCG is 0.523, most-valid-plays NDCG is 0.629, and affinity NDCG is 0.663. The final score does not beat those two simple alternatives in these windows. This is one listener and two correlated held-out windows, not evidence of general model quality. The tuned evaluation weights do not silently replace production weights.

The daily export’s timezone remains unspecified by the source. Exact historical metadata versions cannot be recovered from one export. Notebook execution and pixel-level browser screenshot comparison are **not executed**; the EDA script, data pipeline, and Streamlit widget interactions were executed. No remaining user decision blocks the delivered refactor.
